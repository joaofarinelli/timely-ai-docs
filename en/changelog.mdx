## July 2026 (Jul 1–21)

### Datagrids
- **Kanban view** in datagrids: switch between table and board, group records by column, and drag cards between stages.
- **Redesigned table** with column sorting, pagination, and full-row drag to reorder.
- **Column reordering** by dragging the header, both in the table and in Kanban.
- **New Select field type**, with an options editor, search by option name, and export/import that preserves labels.
- **File columns**: upload images and PDFs straight into the table.
- Add items per stage or through the global button in Kanban, with side-panel editing and board configuration.
- Import and export datagrids in XLSX.
- Kanban cards show creation and update dates, with a tidier footer.
- Various visual and usability refinements to forms, cards, hover, and row selection.

### Orion
- **Autonomous agent building**: Orion plans and creates agents end to end, including missing connections as a step in the plan, with approval and safe resume on failure.
- **Redesigned composer** and a cleaner conversation, with a new reasoning block and web search results.
- Orion creates agents with an HTTP request tool and configures agent tools directly.
- App connection flows resume where they left off after authorization, without repeating steps.
- More consistent responses: no more duplicated blocks, infinite spinners, empty replies, or repeated preliminary messages.
- Clearer error messages when an integration is missing to complete the task.
- The Orion conversation list loads much faster.

### Conversations & Support
- **Ticket info panel** next to the chat, open by default and integrated into the layout.
- Leaner conversation list and a wider chat, making better use of the screen.
- Conversation tags consolidated in the panel's Tags tab, without the top bar.
- Removed the "Take over conversation" button and the warning banner at the top of the conversation.
- Fixed the ticket panel showing up empty for users who belong to multiple workspaces.
- Conversations reconnect on their own when you return to the browser tab.

### Agents & Workers
- **New Assign Tag tool**: the agent tags conversations automatically based on context.
- **Proactive WhatsApp sending** by the agent, without waiting for a customer message.
- In the Workers chat, you can now cancel an in-progress response and edit/resend the last message.
- No more duplicate messages on rapid sends or repeated clicks.
- Google Calendar: full reading of calendars with many items.
- Faster, smoother "thinking" indicator in Orion and Workers.

### Pipeline & CRM
- **Tag-based Kanban replaces pipelines**, with a simpler, more flexible flow for organizing opportunities.
- Fix: cards no longer disappear when moved outside a column.

### Workflows & Automations
- **On-demand webhook testing**, working directly in the draft, with no need to publish.
- Revamped automation engine, with more reliable execution and new execution modes integrated with agents.
- Webhook trigger URL corrected to the permanent address.

### Channels & Integrations
- **Web Page channel**: an evolution of the on-site chat, with more security, cost control, and audio transcription.
- **Slack per channel**: connect different agents to distinct channels in the same workspace, including private channels.
- New native integrations: Google Drive, Google Docs, Discord, Zoom, Intercom, GitLab, Todoist, Resend, Mailchimp, SendGrid, ActiveCampaign, Pipedrive, HighLevel, Figma, Confluence, Box, Coinbase, Hugging Face, Algolia, Lusha, Tavily, ZenRows, DataRobot, and tldv.
- Public chat page fixed and more stable.

### Navigation & Interface
- **New Rovax visual identity**, applied across the platform, emails, widget, and materials.
- **Redesigned global search** in command palette style, also available inside Builder, CRM, and Settings.
- **Smart sidebar**: collapses on detail screens and reopens on hover, with a floating panel and smooth animation.
- New user button, notifications shortcut, and quick access to Help and Support in the sidebar.
- Illustrated empty states across the platform and a new animation and transition system.
- Refreshed icon set and various alignment, spacing, and scrolling refinements.

### Settings
- Profile page rebuilt with cards.
- Workspace grouped with Organizations, Members, and Notifications in a single area.
- Preferences replaced by a dedicated Language page.
- Standardized settings page layout, with no duplicated titles or spacing.

### Notifications
- **Email notifications** for 6 operational events.
- **In-app notification center**, with non-repeating WhatsApp disconnection alerts.

### Builder & Publishing
- Redesigned version selector, with an animated Deploy button, loading indicator, and early validation.
- Deploy is disabled when there are no pending changes.
- Publish dialog with a clearer change comparison.

### Connections
- Connection listing with pagination, filters, multi-select, and bulk delete.
- Significantly faster connection loading.

### Access & Security
- **Email and password sign-in and sign-up** on new, clearer screens.
- Improved data isolation between companies and workspaces.

## June 2026

### White Label
- **Full White Label**: customize the platform with your brand — logo, colors, visual identity, and a login screen of your own, all applied automatically for your customers.
- **Custom domain**: add your own domain, track DNS records and SSL certificate status in real time, and set which domain is primary.
- **Automatic subdomain**: every brand gets its own ready-to-use address, with no manual setup.
- **Emails with your brand**: transactional messages and invitations sent from your verified domain, with customizable templates.
- **Members** and **Billing** tabs inside the White Label environment, with billing and subscription kept on your own domain.
- Customer logo and name shown in the workspace selector, plus an option to remove the "powered by" badge.
- Logo and image uploads are saved automatically while editing the visual identity.

### Orion
- **New Orion**: redesigned chat with a centered, more spacious layout, bubble-free messages, and a revamped home page with an animated greeting.
- **Platform actions**: Orion creates and edits agents, squads, workflows, datagrids, knowledge bases, connections, and campaigns directly from the conversation.
- **Audio recording**: talk to Orion using your microphone and the transcription appears right in the message field.
- **Connect integrations from the conversation**: Orion opens the connection dialog without you leaving the chat.
- **Live reasoning**: follow the agent's step-by-step work during a response, with descriptive labels per action; the history collapses at the end.
- **Planning before acting**: Orion builds a plan and shows it in the activity trail before executing complex tasks.
- **Web search** and **platform documentation lookup** as native tools, for more current and accurate answers.
- Manage an agent's integrations and publish changes just by talking to Orion.
- Conversation history loads faster, switching between conversations no longer loses messages, and the back button no longer leaves ghost conversations behind.

### Agents
- **Builder versioning**: work in a draft and publish when you're ready, without affecting the agent in production.
- **Generative UI**: agents respond with charts, cards, tables, and forms inside the conversation — catalog expanded to 33 components, with incremental editing of the interfaces.
- **Presentations**: agents create HTML decks, with PDF export that preserves slide layout.
- **Terminal**: a new native tool that runs commands in an isolated per-conversation environment.
- **Code execution**: a new tool for calculation and processing in a secure environment, available on all plans.
- **File sending by URL** in agent responses.
- **Contextual memory**: agents remember user information across conversations.
- New language models in the catalog, with revised badges.
- Various visual refinements in the agent editor (more compact side menu, avatars, buttons, and spacing).

### Workers
- **Audio recording with transcription** in the Workers chat as well.
- Long runs (5 to 20+ minutes) are no longer interrupted by timeouts.
- No more stuck runs or infinite loading after refreshing the page.
- Switching between sessions no longer loses messages or typing indicators.
- **Contextual memory** available on the Workers page.

### Conversations & Support
- **Global multi-select filter** by attendants, agents, and squads in the conversation list.
- All / Waiting / Mine tabs redesigned as pills, with inline counts.
- More compact conversation list with a cleaner look (revised borders, searches, and headers).
- Fixed two distinct follow-up semantics and improved message delivery.

### Channels & Integrations
- **Manual WhatsApp Cloud API**: connect an official number directly, without needing to sign in through Facebook.
- **When to reply**: a new per-channel filter to define whether the agent responds to text, audio, or both.
- **Telegram**: native transcription of audio and voice messages, typing indicator on the official channel, and a fix for response delivery.
- **WhatsApp**: responses quote the received audio when configured, message quoting fixed, and lower response time.
- **Instagram**: permissions restored (messaging, publishing, and insights) and connection fixes.
- New shortcut to open the add-channel dialog and a responsive channel selector.
- WhatsApp connection status checked more reliably, and connections saved to the correct active workspace.
- Many new integrations available: Salesforce, Monday, Typeform, Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Tasks, YouTube, Facebook, Meta Ads, PostHog, Cal.com, Reddit, Firecrawl, Gamma, OpenWeather, and more.

### Connections
- **New table-based connections page**, following the platform's visual standard.
- **Redesigned new connection dialog**: integration overview, listing in horizontal rows, and animated transitions between steps.
- Simplified credential collection for API key integrations.
- Various visual refinements to icons, sizes, and spacing in the connection flow.

### Marketplace
- **Agent marketplace by vertical**: ready-made templates with pre-configured tools and direct handoff to Orion.
- **Traffic Squad**: a new ready-made package for paid media management.
- Minimalist card redesign, with 3D covers per template, light and dark theme versions, and real integration logos.
- Marketplace promoted to a top-level item in the Studio section.

### Analytics & Dashboard
- **Rebuilt analytics page** with four fixed cards covering the last 6 months.
- **Peak hours**: a new heatmap by day and hour.
- The channels chart now shows the exact name of each integration.
- Faster charts, with brand colors and correct readability in dark mode.

### Billing & Usage
- **Redesigned usage page**: billing cycle period, daily chart, and plan depletion forecast.
- The chart now shows runs instead of credits.
- Fixes in the plan change and checkout flows.
- Resource limits corrected on active plans.

### Navigation & Interface
- **New visual system** for the platform, with a more modern, consistent finish across the entire application.
- **Redesigned workspace selector** and smoother sidebar transitions.
- **Global search (⌘K)**: the current page appears as the first option.
- New global shortcut to sign out (Option+Shift+Q) and a support button in the sidebar footer.
- Light mode fixed across the entire interface.
- Agents, Datagrids, Knowledge, and Workflows tables unified under the same layout.
- Menus, fields, and tooltips with standardized sizes and behaviors.

### Mobile
- **Mobile responsiveness** across the platform (first phase).

### Settings
- **Preferences** becomes the Settings home page.
- **Providers**: a new page for managing AI providers.
- Standardized settings routes, with correct highlighting of the active menu item.

### Other
- Link invitations now work with passwordless sign-in (email code and Google).
- Blocked numbers system removed.

## May 2026

### Orion
- **Full spreadsheet capability**: read, analyze, modify, and create spreadsheets, including chart generation.
- **Campaigns from a spreadsheet**: upload a lead spreadsheet (CSV, XLSX, and XLS) in the chat and Orion assembles a campaign proposal ready for review.
- **Campaign approval**: approve, edit, or reject the proposal in a dedicated card, with full editing of message, segmentation, and scheduling before sending.
- **Real-time campaign control**: start, pause, resume, or cancel campaigns and track sends, replies, and opt-outs.
- **Automatic opt-out**: replies containing opt-out keywords (also in Spanish and French) remove the contact from the campaign; lead replies are routed to the right agent and become conversations.
- **Multiple conversations in parallel**: Orion keeps processing tasks even if you navigate to another conversation, and restores the history when you return.
- **@ mentions** and a **"+" menu** in the message field: reference agents, channels, and integrations, attach files, and reach connectors in one click.
- **New connector catalog**: a dialog with search, real app icons, an indication of what's active, and a details page with overview, connections, and capabilities.
- **Resizable floating window** on all edges, so you can use Orion on top of any screen.
- **Reworked history**: automatically generated conversation titles, a reorganized sidebar, and quick actions per message.
- Create connections within the flow itself, with an automatic return after authorization.
- Action confirmations in natural language, friendly names for each action, and reasoning shown in real time.
- More reliable answers: data checked against the real database, no made-up information, and correct use of the current date and time.
- Company memory feature removed.

### Agents
- **Native image generation**: agents create images in the conversation, with thumbnail preview, full-screen view, and download.
- **Image editing**: upload an image and ask for changes — the agent uses the latest image in the conversation as the base.
- **Self-scheduling agents**: the agent schedules its own follow-up and resumes the conversation at the right time.
- **New conversational panel** with real-time field updates as the configuration is assembled.
- **Faster and more reliable web search** for agents.
- More consistent responses on long tasks: the agent reviews its own reasoning, runs tools in parallel, and recovers from failures on its own.
- Long conversations retain important context, and long responses with integration content are no longer truncated.
- New reasoning indicator, more informative than the old "thinking".
- Removed the "Channels" and "Blocked numbers" tabs from agent settings.

### Broadcasts (Campaigns)
- **Redesigned campaign builder** with numbered steps, option cards, channel tables, a date/time picker, and clearer helper text.
- **Message variables**: automatic suggestions as you type, colored highlighting in the editor, and a new first name variable.
- **Word rotation**: use `{{option1||option2||option3}}` to automatically vary the text on each send.
- **Block protection**: progressive warm-up per number, randomized intervals with human-like pacing, typing simulation, an exact daily limit, pauses between batches, and blocking of identical messages.
- **Delivery and read receipts** reflected in campaign metrics.
- A single time zone per campaign, applied to scheduling, sending windows, and end time.
- Sends start more gradually, with no burst at the beginning of a broadcast.
- The campaign name is now required to save or send.

### Human Support
- **New Human Support module**: teams, tags, segments, groups, and tickets in a complete support structure.
- Ticket creation without requiring a defined team, and a full listing of available tags.
- New design for the open-ticket action configuration panel.

### Workers
- **Chat attachments**: send images, PDFs, Office files, and CSVs in conversations with workers.
- **Resilient streaming**: navigate between screens without losing an in-progress response; errors and interruptions are visible.
- **Redesigned "Start conversation" dialog**: a single grid of agents and squads, with search and a more compact look.
- Progressive history loading, a button to copy the response, and a balance that updates in real time.

### Conversations & Support
- Conversations screen rebuilt with a more organized layout and smoother navigation.
- **Unified customer identity on WhatsApp**: no more duplicate conversations for the same contact; the customer's real name is always preserved.
- Fixes that prevented messages from mixing between conversations on high-volume accounts.
- Faster conversation list with more reliable real-time updates.
- Error messages rewritten in plain language, with standardized notifications across the product.

### Runs & Analytics
- **New Runs page**, with detailed inspection of each agent run.
- **Redesigned run details**: a second-by-second timeline, drag to navigate, a collapsible side panel, and a button to copy the entire run.
- Runs interrupted by a failure recover automatically.
- Analytics menu reorganized and positioned below CRM.

### Channels & Integrations
- **New WhatsApp foundation**, with better connection stability and send-rate control.
- **Instagram** available both as a channel and as an integration, with 20 actions and localized descriptions.
- **Google Ads**, **Google Analytics 4**, and **Meta Ads** available for agents to use.
- Catalogs for **Notion** (27 actions), **Gmail**, **Twitter/X**, **Salesforce**, **HubSpot**, **Zendesk**, **Shopify**, **Stripe**, **GitHub**, **Jira**, **Linear**, **ClickUp**, **Trello**, **Asana**, **Airtable**, **Apollo**, **LinkedIn**, **Google Sheets**, **Mixpanel**, **Microsoft Teams**, **Google Maps**, and **Canva** expanded and revised in PT/EN.
- Edit the connection used by a channel directly in the channel settings; the status now reflects the provider's real health.
- Channel deletion works without errors and is processed in the background; channel error messages fixed.

### Billing & Plans
- **New usage-tier plan model**: pick your plan with a slider of run tiers, with a single product and direct signup — credits have been replaced by a more transparent usage meter.
- **Three-pillar usage view**, with real measurement of consumption per run, per internal activity, and by Orion.
- Plan menu in the header, showing run usage in real time.
- Annual billing now displays the full yearly amount; plans show their commercial name and highlight the current plan.
- Warnings and blocks when you hit the plan limit, with a direct shortcut to upgrade.

### Appearance & Navigation
- **New dark theme**, aligned with the brand design, with better contrast in dialogs and cards.
- **Orion as the product home screen**, with a restructured sidebar.
- **Help button** with WhatsApp support in the sidebar and animated icons.
- Various visual refinements to buttons and dialogs, plus adaptations for small screens.

### Settings & Access
- **Redesigned API keys page**, including the key creation dialog.
- **Availability toggle** for attendants right in the sidebar, with balanced conversation distribution.
- Workspace permissions aligned between what the screen shows and what is enforced.
- Google sign-up unblocked.
