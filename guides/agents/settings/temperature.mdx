---
title: "Temperature"
---

**Temperature** defines the Agent’s level of creativity and variability. <br />
It influences how deterministic or imaginative the responses will be.

| Range | Behavior | Recommended Use |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| 0.00 – 0.3 | Consistent, factual, analytical | Customer support, data analysis |
| 0.4 – 0.7 | Balanced, adaptive | General-purpose assistants |
| 0.8 – 1.0 | Creative, expressive | Marketing, storytelling, ideation |
